Privacy policy

MRR Mobile is published by Alessandro Sappia. This policy describes the iOS and Android app that uses the bundle id and package name lab.asappia.mrrmobile.

Data stored on your device

API keys and secrets you enter for MiningRigRentals accounts are saved in the device secure store (the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore). The app does not upload those credentials to a server operated by the developer.

MiningRigRentals

When you use an account, the app sends requests directly to MiningRigRentals with the credentials stored on your device. That service has its own privacy policy.

Subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple or Google. The app uses RevenueCat to read whether a subscription entitlement is active. RevenueCat receives purchase and entitlement data needed to unlock the app. The app does not receive your full payment card number.

Analytics, crashes, and ads

If you leave product analytics on, the app sends usage events to Mixpanel in the EU. If you leave crash reports on, the app sends crash data to Sentry with email, username, and IP removed. Meta (Facebook) ad measurement stays off unless you allow app tracking. You can change these in the app under Privacy. The app does not show ads, and it does not sell personal information.

Contact

Email a.sappia@gmail.com to ask a privacy question or request help with data stored on your device.
